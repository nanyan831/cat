# CatLifePet CURIOUS Asset Notes

- Eight independent 512 x 512 RGBA PNG frames.
- Transparent background; alpha bottom anchored to `cat_idle_01.png` at y=451.
- Sequence: IDLE, early attention, gentle head tilt, stronger head tilt, curious hold,
  early recovery, near-neutral recovery, IDLE recovery.
- Ears and gaze lead the motion while the seated paws remain visually planted.
- Frames were generated with the built-in image tool using CatLifePet IDLE and the
  approved curious key pose as identity references, then chroma-keyed and normalized.
- Runtime target: ONCE, 1620ms frame duration plus a small state-completion margin.
